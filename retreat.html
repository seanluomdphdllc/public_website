New York Physician Dad's Retreat

Who are you?

I am a board-certified academic addiction psychiatrist who conducts research in addiction data science at Columbia University and runs a small private practice in general and addiction psychiatry on the Upper West Side. You can find my biography here.


What is this?

This is a short retreat program (Sat-Sun) for a small group of physician fathers in the tri-state area.


What makes this program content unique?

We restrict entry for the time being to physician dads who live around New York. This criterion allows for a common language of shared experiences that provide a foundation for the retreat.

The retreat program also focuses on disseminating an evidence-based knowledge base, including neuroscience and clinical trial interventions, that have proven efficacy in treating mental illness. The goal of the program is not treatment, but restructure “physician wellness” around sound scientific evidence.


Stylistically, how is the program different?

Physician wellness programs are typically either 1) driven by large organizations to try to make physicians work harder at all costs or 2) driven by the wellness industry to get physicians to consume and stop working. Physician dads typically do not endorse either of these ulterior agendas. The wellness industry has also had an existing focus that neglects men, and in particular fathers, who may experience unique challenges. Stylistically, many wellness programs either overuse G-rated corporate wellness jargons or become therapy groups. This program aims to integrate mindfulness with radical transparency and speak in plain, R-rated language.

The depth of scientific content and case scenarios can be anxiety provoking and intense. Designed for physician dads with some mental focus and fortitude, the program is not suitable for individuals with active, untreated mental illness or substance use problems, but we can provide referral if after the program you decide to seek further treatment.


What is the program structure?

Sat
Arrival at the retreat center at around 3PM.
Silent guided meditation between 5 and 5:45.
Short break.
6PM-7PM Dinner
7PM-8PM Workshop 1: Effective cognitive control for career and financial success
15 min brief introduction on
- Neuroscience of cognitive control
- Mindfulness and cognitive control
- Setting goals and resolving conflicts
- Action, evaluation
45 min participant discussion
8:30PM-9:15PM Self-directed meditation

Sun
Sleeping in and check out, exercise, etc

10AM-10:30AM Walking meditation
10:30AM-11:30AM Workshop 2: Dopamine and impulsive behavior
15 min introduction on
-Neuroscience of addiction
-Drugs, alcohol, sex, food, video games, pornography, gambling, other externalizing behaviors
-Mindfulness, cognitive control, and remediation
-Advanced topics: pharmacotherapy, PHP, recovery groups, ethics
45 min participant discussion
PM – lunch, use of facility, wellness therapy, etc.

Other administrative details?

The program advocates a vegetarian non-alcohol weekend and light exercise, as well as unrestricted sleep. It will happen at a facility a short drive away from Manhattan. Carpooling is encouraged.

We are working on getting this program eligible for Category 1 CME. It is eligible for Category 2 CME.


What is your confidentiality policy?

Given that the discussion material and case scenarios are sensitive and protected information, all content shared with the group is kept strictly confidential within the group. We also discourage sharing any sensitive content with anyone else outside of the group. While this does impart a Fight Clue vibe, it ensures that judgement can be exercised with some guideline.

In particular, we will discuss the complexities and ambiguities surrounding these issues in Workshop 2.
