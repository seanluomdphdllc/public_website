汉字卡片Chinese Word Cards · simplified characters with pinyin

Three decks for conversation: characters for reading, words and phrases for speaking, and sentence frames that turn words into sentences.

Pinyin is hidden by default on purpose: children who read the pinyin often stop reading the characters. Tap a card or the Show pinyin button when you want it. Audio uses your browser's Mandarin voice, which works best in Chrome, Edge, and Safari. Stars and scores are saved in this browser only.
