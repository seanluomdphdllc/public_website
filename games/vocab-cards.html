Word Cards

Vocabulary from grade 5 (ISEE Lower Level) through the SAT, with pronunciation, meanings, and sentences from classic writers.

Words beyond the SAT: literary, old-fashioned, and seldom-tested words for strong readers and word lovers. You won't need most of these for any exam.

Quoted sentences come from public-domain classics and historical documents. Cards without a named source use an original example sentence. Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in voice. Stars and scores are saved in this browser only.
