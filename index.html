Welcome

Sean X. Luo, M.D., Ph.D., LLC is a boutique general and addiction psychiatry practice located on the Upper West Side, New York.

As an addiction psychiatrist, I provide leading edge scientifically informed treatment for substance use disorders, alcoholism and other addictions. I treat these conditions as well as underlying psychiatric co-morbidities such as Attention Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) and personality disorders. I design a treatment plan informed by the latest neuroscience breakthroughs and discoveries. I also work with patients and families to build a system of care to target multiple dimensions in recovery.

My consulting practice focuses on applications of current research in clinical neuroscience, addiction science, data science, and machine learning in mental health technology and product development, as well as quantitative systems design, predictive modeling, and quality evaluation in substance use disorder treatment. I engage clients in financial services, venture capital, startup companies, pharmaceutical companies, legal services, and related areas.

I provide high-definition telemedicine services to patients in New York, New Jersey and California, and conduct international consultations.
